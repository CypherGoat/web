### Recent News

- P2Pool v4.16 was released with a critical consensus vulnerability fix; all users are strongly urged to update immediately. [Release](https://github.com/SChernykh/p2pool/releases/tag/v4.16)
- A wallet with no prior history received $120M in USDT, routed it through exchanges and instant swaps into Monero (moving the price 27% to a high of $438), and had $72M subsequently frozen by Tether. [Monero Wire](https://monerowire.com/articles/tether-72m-usdt-freeze-120m-tron-monero.html)
- xmr-pay, a free open-source serverless and non-custodial Monero payment gateway requiring no backend infrastructure, was released for community testing. [r/Monero](https://www.reddit.com/r/Monero/comments/1u3kl74/xmrpay_free_open_source_monero_payment_gateway/)
- Cypher eSIM added Monero support to its no-KYC eSIM service, with XMR now accepted on both web checkout and mobile. [r/Monero](https://www.reddit.com/r/Monero/comments/1u6kidd/added_monero_support_to_our_nokyc_esim_service/)
