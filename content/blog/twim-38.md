### Recent News

- Cake Labs released Cake Wallet v6.4.4 with native Trezor support for Bitcoin, smoother Monero syncing, and bug fixes. [Release](https://monero.observer/cake-wallet-monero.com-v6.4.4-released-monero-sync-improvements/)
- everoddandeven released Monero Python Library v0.9.3 with monero-cpp updates and test/typing maintenance fixes. [Release](https://monero.observer/everoddandeven-releases-monero-python-library-v0.9.3/)
- selsta submitted a new CCS proposal to work full-time on Monero development for 3 more months, through the end of November 2026. [Source](https://monero.observer/selsta-full-time-monero-dev-ccs-proposal-september-november-2026/)
- Boog900's CCS proposal to integrate FCMP++ into Cuprate is ready for funding, requesting 85 XMR. [Source](https://monero.observer/boog900-ccs-cuprate-fcmp-support-ready-funding/)
- DangerousFreedom1984 posted the final progress report for their CCS proposal to integrate FCMP++ into Monero Inflation Checker, closing out the work. [Source](https://monero.observer/dangerousfreedom1984-completes-moneroinflation-fcmps-ccs/)
- The next Monero Research Lab meeting is scheduled for Wednesday, September 9th 2026 at 17:00 UTC to discuss FCMP++ status and relative locks. [Source](https://monero.observer/monero-research-lab-meeting-9-september-2026/)
