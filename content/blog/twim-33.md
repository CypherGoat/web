### Recent News

- SEC Commissioner Hester Peirce defended privacy-enhancing technologies at Georgetown Law, arguing financial privacy "does not conflict with national security objectives" — a direct contrast with EU proposals to restrict privacy coins by 2027. [Source](https://cointelegraph.com/news/sec-hester-peirce-defends-crypto-privacy-tools-surveillance)
- The EU's 20th sanctions package bans all Russian and Belarusian crypto providers, with the digital ruble sanctioned before it even launched. [Source](https://monerowire.com/articles/eu-bans-russian-belarusian-crypto-providers.html)
- Community members documented a wave of Monero ecosystem vulnerabilities following the Haveno protocol exploit, attributing the spike partly to advances in commercial LLMs. [Source](https://www.reddit.com/r/Monero/comments/1tp68w3/why_so_many_vulnerabilties_recently/)
- XMR.Bar announced the launch of XMR.WIN, a Monero-native prediction market, on Monerotopia episode 264. [Source](https://www.monerotalk.live/monerotopia-264)
- A developer shared a follow-up test of a self-custody Monero payments system, addressing community feedback about the previous centralized approach. [Source](https://v.redd.it/6o59hptomx3h1)
- A user in Houston paid at a restaurant with XMR in person, reporting instant confirmation on the merchant's tablet. [Source](https://www.reddit.com/gallery/1tp5ohf)
- MoneroKon 6 takes place June 5–7 in Warsaw, Poland — CypherGoat is speaking on June 6 with a talk titled "Liquidity Fragmentation in Non-Custodial XMR Markets". **If you're attending, we'll also be running a scavenger hunt with XMR and swag up for grabs.** [Details](https://monerokon.org) [Hunt Details](https://cyphergoat.com/monerokon2026)
