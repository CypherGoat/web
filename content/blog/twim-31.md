### Recent News

- CypherGoat launched a live transparency page showing KYC incident rates, on-chain verifiable security deposits, and open-source commit history. [Source](https://www.reddit.com/r/Monero/comments/1tc9hbd/cyphergoat_now_publishes_a_live_transparency_page/)
- Monero v0.18.5.0 "Fluorine Fermi, Major Point Release 5" released, adding SOCKS v5 proxy support, removing UPnP, and including numerous bug fixes. [Release](https://github.com/monero-project/monero/releases/tag/v0.18.5.0)
- P2Pool v4.15.1 released, fixing a rare invalid-hash bug (1 in ~268 million) on ARM/RISC-V systems. [Release](https://github.com/SChernykh/p2pool/releases/tag/v4.15.1)
- The Monero Research Lab shared a post-quantum encryption progress update. [Source](https://x.com/monero/status/2052822863669858358)
- Plaintext podcast Episode 1 covers Generalized Bulletproofs with cryptographer Luke Parker. [Source](https://youtu.be/EckeY4TsNkU)
