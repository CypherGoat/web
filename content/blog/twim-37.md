### Recent News

- Trail of Bits completed its audit of Monero's FCMP++ cryptography implementation and reported zero high, medium, or low-severity findings, with six informational findings, five already resolved. [Source](https://monero.observer/trail-of-bits-audits-monero-fcmps-cryptography-implementation/)
- THORChain says it is "a few weeks" away from full XMR activation following its v3.20 protocol update, which builds on the v3.19 foundations for Monero support. [Source](https://monero.observer/thorchain-few-weeks-away-from-monero-integration/)
- Cuprate released v0.1.0-preview "Kesterite," the first version supporting wallet syncing and transaction broadcasting, with early comparisons showing 2x faster genesis sync than monerod on the same hardware. [Source](https://www.reddit.com/r/Monero/comments/1vcx58x/cuprate_v010preview_kesterite_released_you_can/)
- Cake Labs released Cake Wallet & Monero.com v6.4.1, adding Zcash enhancements, an improved AnyPay flow, UI updates, and bug fixes. [Release](https://monero.observer/cake-wallet-monero.com-v6.4.1-released/)
- SChernykh released P2Pool v4.18, optimizing Stratum server performance to support up to 100,000 connections. [Release](https://github.com/SChernykh/p2pool/releases/tag/v4.18)
- j-berman published a third progress report on his full-time 2026 CCS proposal after 562 hours of Monero/FCMP++ and Carrot dev work, noting all Phase 1 audit integration PRs have merged and FCMP++/Carrot beta stressnet v3 is coming soon. [Source](https://monero.observer/j-berman-posts-ccs-progress-report-562-hours-dev-work/)
